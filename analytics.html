Analytics & GA4

Measurement you can actually trust

A clean GA4 setup, meaningful events, and reports built around decisions — not a dashboard nobody opens.

GA4's event-based model is more flexible than the old session-based tools it replaced, but that flexibility only pays off if the underlying setup is deliberate. A property with unfiltered internal traffic, inconsistent event names, or no defined conversions produces reports that look precise and mean very little.

This section starts with getting the foundation right — property structure, data streams, and conversions — then moves into instrumenting events that reflect what users actually do, understanding who your best-performing audience segments are, and building reports around the handful of questions stakeholders actually ask instead of a wall of default charts.
