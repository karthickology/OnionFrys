Design

Design that earns its opinions

UI/UX principles, portfolio presentation, and the case studies that show the thinking behind the pixels.

Good design decisions repeat across projects even when the visual style doesn't: establishing hierarchy before decoration, keeping patterns consistent enough that people stop having to relearn an interface, and giving feedback for every action so a user is never left guessing whether something worked.

This section covers those UI/UX fundamentals, then turns to presentation — how to structure a portfolio and write case studies that make the thinking behind a project visible, not just the final screenshot. A design case study is only convincing when it's honest about the constraints it was made under and the alternatives it ruled out.
