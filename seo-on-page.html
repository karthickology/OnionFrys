On-Page SEO

Structuring a page so intent, hierarchy, and crawlability are obvious at a glance.

On this page

  1. Title tags and meta descriptions that earn the click
  2. Heading hierarchy and content structure
  3. Internal linking with intent
  4. Media and accessibility

Title tags and meta descriptions that earn the click

A title tag has one job beyond ranking: getting clicked in a results page full of competitors. That means a unique, specific title for every page — never a template repeated with one word swapped — with the primary keyword appearing naturally near the front.

Meta descriptions don't directly affect rankings, but they're the closest thing to ad copy you get in organic search. Write them like a reason to click, not a keyword-stuffed summary.

Heading hierarchy and content structure

One H1 per page, and headings that nest logically underneath it — H3s under the H2 they belong to, never skipping a level. This isn't just a crawler preference; it's how a scanning reader (which is most readers) understands a page's shape in two seconds.

Internal linking with intent

Anchor text should describe the destination page, not just say "click here." Link to your priority pages from wherever it's genuinely relevant — internal links are one of the few ranking signals entirely within your control, and they're how authority flows from your strongest pages to newer ones.

Media and accessibility

Every image needs alt text that describes its content, both for accessibility and because search engines can't see an image, only read about it. Image weight also matters for ranking through Core Web Vitals — see the Performance section for the technical side of that.

Compounding, not competing

On-page work and technical SEO reinforce each other. A perfectly written page still won't rank if a crawler can't reach it — check the Technical SEO cluster alongside this one.

Action checklist

  • ✓Write a unique title and meta description for every page
  • ✓Confirm exactly one H1 exists per page
  • ✓Check heading levels don't skip (H2 → H4 with no H3)
  • ✓Add descriptive alt text to every meaningful image
  • ✓Link to at least two other relevant pages from any new page
