Paid acquisition that's accountable to a number, not just impressions.

Structuring campaigns around intent

Search ads catch existing demand (someone already searching for a solution); display and social ads create awareness of demand that doesn't exist yet. Treating them the same — same landing page, same message, same budget logic — wastes spend on both.

Budgeting and bidding basics

Starting with a small, controlled budget and a manual or conservative automated bid strategy gives you a clean read on performance before scaling. Scaling spend on an unproven campaign just multiplies whatever mistake is already in it.

Landing pages built for the ad, not the homepage

A generic homepage is rarely the right destination for a paid click — message match (the ad's promise reflected immediately on the landing page) is one of the more reliable levers on conversion rate and, on some platforms, on cost per click too.

Tracking that survives a platform update

Conversion tracking tied to a single ad platform is fragile. Feeding conversions into GA4 as the source of truth, and treating platform-reported numbers as a second opinion, keeps measurement stable even when a platform's own attribution changes.

Prove it small before you scale it

Every dollar spent on an unproven campaign is a dollar spent finding out whether it works — budget for that, and don't scale until it has.

Action checklist

  • ✓Match landing page message to ad copy before launch
  • ✓Start new campaigns with a controlled budget before scaling
  • ✓Set up conversion tracking into GA4, not just the ad platform's own dashboard
  • ✓Separate awareness-stage campaigns from intent-stage campaigns in reporting
  • ✓Review search terms reports regularly to catch irrelevant matches
