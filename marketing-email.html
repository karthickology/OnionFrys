Email Marketing

The one channel where you own the relationship outright — no algorithm in between.

List building that doesn't annoy anyone

"A lead magnet works best when it maps directly to real intent. Someone who wants a template should get a template—not a generic newsletter signup. The more specific and relevant the offer, the more likely they are to trust you and actually engage.

Clarity about what someone's opting into keeps list quality high and unsubscribe rates low. Be upfront about what they'll get and how often. No surprises, no bait-and-switch—just honest value that matches their expectations."

Welcome and nurture sequences

"The first three to five emails after a signup do more work than almost anything that follows. They're your best chance to set expectations, show you deliver on what was promised, and build trust early—while the person still remembers why they joined.

Use this window to deliver on the reason they signed up, introduce what your broader offer looks like, and give them a taste of your value—without leading with a hard sell. Get this right, and they'll stay engaged for the long haul. Rush it or pitch too soon, and they'll check out fast."

Campaigns vs. automations

"A campaign is a one-off send tied to a specific moment—a product launch, an update, a seasonal offer. It's timely, intentional, and happens when you have something new to say. An automation runs on a trigger and keeps working without manual sends—welcome sequences, abandoned cart reminders, or post-purchase follow-ups.

Most lists need both: automations for the predictable moments that happen again and again, and campaigns for everything else. One builds the machine; the other makes it human."

Deliverability basics

"None of this matters if your email lands in spam. You can have the best content and perfect timing, but if you don't reach the inbox, nothing else counts. Getting flagged as spam kills your open rates and your credibility fast.

Proper authentication (SPF, DKIM, DMARC), a clean sender reputation maintained by removing inactive subscribers, and consistent sending patterns all affect whether an inbox trusts your domain. Stay on top of these, and your emails actually get seen."

Own the list, respect the list

A list is the one audience asset a platform change can't take away from you — treat every send as a reason to stay on it, not just a reason to open it.

Action checklist

  • ✓Match every lead magnet to a specific, stated intent
  • ✓Build a welcome sequence before driving traffic to a signup form
  • ✓Separate evergreen automations from one-off campaigns
  • ✓Set up SPF, DKIM, and DMARC before sending at volume
  • ✓Prune inactive subscribers on a regular schedule
