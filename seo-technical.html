Technical SEO

The invisible work that decides whether your content gets evaluated at all.

Crawlability and indexation

"Robots.txt tells search engines which parts of your site they can and can't crawl. A correct sitemap.xml shows them all the pages you want them to find. Together, they guide search engines to your best content.

But mistakes happen—like misconfigured noindex tags, accidental robots.txt blocks, or orphaned pages (pages with no internal links pointing to them). These are some of the most common—and invisible—reasons a page never ranks. Check your setup regularly so your best pages actually get found."

Site architecture

"A flat site structure keeps every page just a few clicks from the homepage. This helps visitors find things easily and makes canonical tags simpler to manage. A logical site hierarchy means cleaner organization and happier users.

Canonical tags matter most when the same content is reachable through multiple URLs—like with filters, sorting, or tracking parameters. Without one, search engines have to guess which version to show. A canonical tag tells them the master version, so all the link authority stays in one place."

Structured data

"Schema markup (usually JSON-LD) doesn't boost rankings directly, but it makes your content eligible for rich results—like star ratings, FAQ dropdowns, and breadcrumb trails in search results. These eye-catching extras help your listing stand out and get more clicks.

For most sites, a few types cover everything: Article schema for blog posts, FAQ schema for question-and-answer sections, Breadcrumb schema for site navigation, and Product schema for items you sell. Add the right schema, and your search listing becomes more useful and more clickable."

Core Web Vitals as a ranking input

"Page experience—measured largely through Core Web Vitals—is one of the few technical ranking factors Google has directly confirmed. It's about how fast your page loads, how stable it feels, and how responsive it is to interactions.

We cover it fully in the Performance section, but it belongs on every technical SEO checklist. Good page experience keeps visitors happy and helps your rankings too."

Fix the foundation first

No amount of content or link building overcomes a page a crawler can't reach or won't index. When rankings stall unexpectedly, check the technical basics before anything else.

Action checklist

  • ✓Confirm robots.txt isn't blocking pages that should be indexed
  • ✓Submit an accurate, up-to-date sitemap.xml
  • ✓Check for orphaned pages with no internal links pointing to them
  • ✓Add canonical tags anywhere the same content is reachable via multiple URLs
  • ✓Validate structured data with a schema testing tool before publishing
