Analytics & GA4

GA4 Setup

The foundation work that determines whether every report after this one can be trusted.

On this page

  2. Connecting Search Console and Ads

Property and data stream basics

"One property per site keeps your reporting clean and easy to manage. A data stream is where you actually configure the tracking code and settings for a given site or app—this is where the technical setup happens.

Data retention settings are easy to overlook and default to a shorter window than most teams expect. If you don't adjust them early, you could lose historical data faster than you realize. Worth checking on day one, before you start collecting data you might later wish you still had."

Connecting Search Console and Ads

"Linking Search Console surfaces organic query data directly inside GA4 reports—so you can see exactly which search terms are driving traffic alongside user behavior. Linking your ads account brings cost and campaign data in alongside on-site behavior, so you can judge paid performance against the same conversions as everything else.

One unified view of organic, paid, and on-site data helps you make smarter decisions—without jumping between platforms or guessing what's actually working."

Defining conversions early

"Mark key events as conversions before a campaign launches, not after. Getting this setup done early ensures you capture every conversion from day one. Too many teams realize too late that they forgot to configure tracking properly.

Retroactively marking a conversion doesn't backfill historical data the way many people assume. If a campaign ran before this step was done, those early conversions are effectively lost—you'll never see them in your reports. Set it up first, measure everything, and avoid the blind spot."

Common setup mistakes

"Unfiltered internal or office traffic, a missing cross-domain configuration for multi-domain checkouts, and unconfigured referral exclusions are the most common reasons a GA4 property reports numbers that look plausible but aren't accurate. They quietly distort your data without breaking anything obvious—so you don't notice until decisions go wrong.

Filter out your own team's visits, connect domains that belong to the same checkout flow, and exclude payment gateways as referrers. Fix these early, and your numbers start telling the truth from day one."

Fix it before, not after

Every day a GA4 property runs with a setup mistake is a day of data that can't be corrected retroactively. Audit the basics before trusting a single report.

Action checklist

  • ✓Confirm data retention settings match how far back you'll need to look
  • ✓Link Search Console and any ad accounts in use
  • ✓Mark key conversion events before launching a campaign
  • ✓Filter out internal and office traffic
  • ✓Verify cross-domain tracking if checkout or booking happens on a different domain
